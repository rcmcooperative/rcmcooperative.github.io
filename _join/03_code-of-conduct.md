Our Code of Conduct sets out how we engage with each other, across the co-op and any spaces we facilitate. We're committed to a welcoming, harassment-free environment for everyone, and we take a restorative approach when things go wrong, focusing on repairing harm and rebuilding trust rather than punishment. Concerns can be raised directly with our Directors, and all reports are handled with care and confidentiality.

**Full details of our [Code of Conduct including reporting lines are available here](https://github.com/rcmcooperative/governance/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)**
