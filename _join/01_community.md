Open to anyone who wants to stay connected to the RCM Cooperative and the wider practice of Research Community Management. 

Community membership will be offered to anyone who has either:
1. Attended at least two events in a year, and/or;
2. Demonstrated sustained engagement and contribution to RCM Cooperative the via our communication channels for a minimum of three months.

Once the membership offer is accepted, it is valid for 1 year.

Community members vote on high strategic decisions such as elections the Board, approving changes to our constitution, rules and governing policies, special resolutions and profit distribution. Every Community Member gets one vote. All Community Member votes combined count for 25% of the total outcome on each voting decision.

**Please contact [cassandra.gouldvanpraag@rcmcooperative.com](mailto:cassandra.gouldvanpraag@rcmcooperative.com) to discuss joining RCM Cooperative as a Community Member!**
